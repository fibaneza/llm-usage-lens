# App Teams Spec — App teams and LiteLLM teams as separate concepts

Decided 2026-11-17 (user). Amends the
[LiteLLM spec](2026-11-10-litellm.md); where the two disagree on the points
below, this file wins until unit L4b folds it in (task 1).

## Overview

LiteLLM limits how many teams can be created, so people from several real
teams share one LiteLLM team. The dashboard's own teams (**app teams**) solve
that: an app team is managed in the dashboard, independently of LiteLLM, and
its LiteLLM spend is available whether or not a LiteLLM team stands behind it.

User direction (2026-11-17): "The app must manage app teams and litellm team
separated. … Team lead should able to see the team spend as sumup of user
spend individually. But the app admin and org admin should able to see the
spend of the litellm team and app team. … If the relationship is linked then
app could use the litellm team spend api otherwise sum up member spend
regardless the team spend."

## Vocabulary

| Term             | Meaning                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **App team**     | A dashboard team (`teamId`). Membership is the Access Control affiliation: at most one app team per person                     |
| **LiteLLM team** | A team on the LiteLLM proxy (`team_id`). Membership, budgets, limits and keys live in LiteLLM                                  |
| **Linked**       | An app team with a `TeamLink` to its own LiteLLM team, one-to-one (LiteLLM spec — _Identity mapping and members_). Unchanged   |
| **Unlinked**     | An app team with no `TeamLink`. Its members may sit in any LiteLLM team, including one shared with other app teams, or in none |
| **Member sum**   | The sum of the LiteLLM-reported spend of each app-team member, read per LiteLLM user                                           |

UI copy says "team" only for an app team and always "LiteLLM team" for the
proxy's.

## What does not change

- App membership and LiteLLM membership stay separate writes. Adding or
  removing an app member never calls LiteLLM (LiteLLM spec — _Identity
  mapping and members_).
- `TeamLink` stays one-to-one in both directions, with its claim item. No new
  entity, no new key pattern, no migration: **the absence of a link is the
  flag** for member-sum mode.
- A linked team keeps every L3 read and L4 write exactly as specified.
- The gateway allowlist gains no operation. Two _Who_ columns widen
  (_Allowlist changes_).

## Spend source

| App team | `spendSource`  | Headline spend                                        | LiteLLM controls on the Team page          |
| -------- | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Linked   | `litellm-team` | `getTeam` and `getTeamDailySpend`, as today           | All L4 controls, as today                  |
| Unlinked | `member-sum`   | Member sum over the date range (_Member sum_, below) | None. App membership add and remove only |

- **Lead** (in scope): sees the headline for the team's source. On a linked
  team that is the LiteLLM team spend (AT1).
- **Admin, org-admin, Auditor**: see the headline, and on a linked team
  **also** the member sum as a second figure, on request
  (`?include=memberSum`), with the note in _Why the figures differ_.
- An unlinked team no longer shows "not connected to LiteLLM" as an empty
  state. It shows its member-sum spend and one line: "This team has no
  LiteLLM team of its own. Budgets, limits and keys are managed in LiteLLM."

### Member sum

Computed in one server-only module, `lib/litellm/member-sum.ts`:

1. **Members** are the bound affiliations of the app team
   (`listAllTeamMemberOids`). Pending affiliations are counted, not summed.
2. **LiteLLM user** for each member: `IdentityLink.litellmUserId`; when
   absent, `findUserByEmail` on the identity's verified email (the LD13 and
   LD26 pattern: read from LiteLLM, never composed). No link is written.
3. **Spend** for each matched member: `getUserDailySpend` for the range,
   `timezone=0` (LD20). Rows are added per UTC day; the total is the sum of
   the days.
4. **Coverage** is always returned and shown:
   `{ members, matched, unmatched, pending }`. An unmatched member is listed
   as "not in LiteLLM"; it is never shown as $0.
5. **Bounds.** At most 5 concurrent LiteLLM reads (`runBounded`,
   `packages/shared`). A team with more than 200 members returns
   `422 TOO_MANY_MEMBERS` with no LiteLLM call (AT6). LiteLLM reads stay
   uncached (LiteLLM spec — _Caching_).
6. **Failure.** If any member read fails, the route returns its standard
   LiteLLM upstream error; a partial sum is never returned (Rule 60.17, AT7).
7. **Toggles.** `getUserDailySpend` disabled → `403 OPERATION_DISABLED`, as
   for every read.

### Why the figures differ

Shown wherever both figures appear:

- The **LiteLLM team** figure is everything spent through that LiteLLM team's
  keys, including keys owned by nobody in the app team.
- The **member sum** is everything each app member spent through the gateway,
  in any LiteLLM team and on personal keys.
- Membership is read as it is now. A person who moved app team carries their
  earlier spend to the new team for any date range (residual risk).

Both are **LiteLLM-reported**, not billed cost (Rule 20.3; LiteLLM spec —
_Cost visualisation and provenance_). USD, UTC days.

### Organisation cap

Per-user LiteLLM cost stays hidden while `scope.userLevelCap` is below `all`
(LD23): the member rows then carry no `spend`. **The team's member sum is
always shown, whatever the member count** (AT3, user decision). On a team of
one or two members the sum reveals an individual's cost; this is accepted and
listed under _Residual risks_.

## Organisation view

Amends LiteLLM spec — _Views by role_ (_Organisation_). Two tables, because
the two cuts do not add up to each other:

| Table             | Rows                                         | Columns                                                         | Source                                                                               |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **LiteLLM teams** | Every team on the proxy                      | Alias, spend, budget, members, linked app team (or "unlinked") | `listProxyTeams`, then one `getTeamDailySpend` with those ids, and `getTeam` per row |
| **App teams**     | Every app team                               | Name, spend source, spend, coverage                             | Linked: the LiteLLM team row. Unlinked: member sum                                   |

- **The headline total is the LiteLLM teams table's total**, never a sum of
  app teams: a member of an unlinked team can spend through a LiteLLM team
  linked to another app team and would be counted twice (AT5).
- The app teams table is labelled "Per app team. Rows overlap with each other
  and do not add up to the total."
- **Read budget.** One Organisation request makes at most 200 member reads in
  total. Unlinked teams are summed smallest first; a team that does not fit
  returns `spend: null` with `reason: "open-team"`, and the row links to the
  Team page.

## Allowlist changes

No new operation, method, path or query. _Who_ columns only:

| Operation           | Today                                                    | Becomes                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `listProxyTeams`    | Admin (link picker); the connection test                 | Also org-admin and Auditor, for the Organisation view's LiteLLM teams table                 |
| `getUserDailySpend` | Viewer (self), Lead, Admin, Auditor                      | Unchanged roles; a Lead's members are the app team's members, whether or not it is linked |
| `findUserByEmail`   | Server only                                              | Also the member-sum lookup for a member with no stored link                                 |

The structure test (`lib/litellm/structure.test.ts`) must still pass with the
same operation set.

## API changes

No new route. Response shapes are Zod schemas in `packages/shared`, validated
on both ends (Rule 10.4).

| Route                                 | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/litellm/teams/[teamId]`     | Always returns `spendSource`. Unlinked: `connected: false`, `daily`, and `memberSum: { total, daily, coverage, members }`. Linked: as today, plus `memberSum` when `include=memberSum` and the caller is Admin, org-admin or Auditor (ignored otherwise) |
| `GET /api/litellm/org`                | Returns `totals`, `daily`, `litellmTeams[]` and `appTeams[]` per _Organisation view_. `teams[]` is removed                                                                                                                                         |
| `GET /api/litellm/teams/[teamId]/*`   | Unchanged: on an unlinked team the L4 reads return `connected: false` and the L4 writes return their existing not-connected refusal                                                                                                               |

`memberSum.members[]` is `{ oid, name?, matched, spend? }`; `spend` is absent
when the Organisation cap hides per-user cost.

Scoping is unchanged: `resolveLiteLLMTeam` decides who may read a team, and
an out-of-scope team returns the same `404` as a missing one (Access Control
— _Enforcement_ item 2).

## UI

- **Team page** (Management → Team): a source badge beside the headline
  ("LiteLLM team" or "Sum of members"); the coverage line; on an unlinked
  team the member table (name, "not in LiteLLM" where unmatched, spend) with
  app membership add and remove, and no LiteLLM control. For Admin, org-admin
  and Auditor on a linked team, a second figure "Sum of members" with the
  _Why the figures differ_ text.
- **Organisation page**: the two tables above.
- **Settings → LiteLLM → Team links**: a "Spend source" column for every app
  team.
- Design tokens, Suspense and error boundaries per Rule 40. No new chart
  type.

## Decisions

| ID  | Question                                                  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AT1 | A Lead's headline on a linked team                        | **LiteLLM team spend** (user, 2026-11-17: the recommendation; "if the relationship is linked then app could use the litellm team spend api")                                                                                                                 |
| AT2 | Who sets the link                                         | **Admin and org-admin only**, as LD24 (user, 2026-11-17: the recommendation). A Lead who could declare a link would gain read and write over a LiteLLM team it may not own                                                                                    |
| AT3 | Small teams under the Organisation cap                    | **Always show the member sum** (user, 2026-11-17: "always show it")                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| AT4 | LiteLLM writes for an unlinked team                       | **None in this unit** (agent decision, 2026-11-17, delegated by the user: "not sure, you decide"). The members' keys and budgets sit in a LiteLLM team shared with people outside the Lead's scope, and the key ownership check (`/key/info` `team_id` equals the linked team) has no equivalent there. A "shared LiteLLM team" mode is recorded in `docs/future-improvements.md` |
| AT5 | Organisation total                                        | **From the LiteLLM teams**, never a sum of app teams (agent decision, 2026-11-17: avoids double counting; reversible)                                                                                                                                          |
| AT6 | Bounds on the member sum                                  | **5 concurrent reads, 200 members per team, 200 member reads per Organisation request** (agent decision, 2026-11-17: common practice, reversible; `/user/daily/activity` takes one `user_id` in contract 1.109.1)                                             |
| AT7 | A member read fails                                       | **The request fails**; no partial sum (agent decision, 2026-11-17: Rule 60.17, reversible)                                                                                                                                                                     |

Task 1 gives these the next free `LD` numbers in the LiteLLM spec.

## Threat model

| ID   | Threat                                                                        | Control                                                                                                                             |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ATT1 | A Lead reads the spend of people outside its teams through the member sum     | Members come only from the affiliations of a team `resolveLiteLLMTeam` allowed; out of scope is `404`                               |
| ATT2 | A wrong identity match adds someone else's spend                              | The LiteLLM user id is the stored link or `findUserByEmail` on the verified email, never composed; a result with another email is dropped |
| ATT3 | The fan-out overloads LiteLLM                                                 | AT6 bounds; `422` above the member cap with no LiteLLM call                                                                         |
| ATT4 | A Lead links its team to a LiteLLM team it does not own                       | AT2: link writes stay Admin and org-admin; unchanged `403` for a Lead                                                               |
| ATT5 | A double-counted total misleads a reader                                      | AT5 and the label on the app teams table                                                                                            |
| ATT6 | `include=memberSum` gives a Lead a figure it should not have on a linked team | The parameter is ignored unless the caller is Admin, org-admin or Auditor                                                           |

## Residual risks

- On a team of one or two members the member sum equals or nearly equals one
  person's cost, even under the Organisation cap (AT3, accepted by the user).
- Membership has no history: a mover's earlier spend follows them. A daily
  snapshot would fix this and needs a scheduler; recorded in
  `docs/future-improvements.md`.
- A large unlinked team costs one LiteLLM read per member on every view.
